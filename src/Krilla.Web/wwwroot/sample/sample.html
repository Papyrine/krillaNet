Converting HTML to PDF

This page was laid out and painted by krilla, compiled to WebAssembly and running inside the browser. Nothing was uploaded anywhere. offline

Floats shorten lines, not blocks. A block beside a float keeps its full width and simply overlaps it — only the lines inside it wrap. This box is a float, and the paragraphs beside it are demonstrating exactly that.

The engine implements block and inline layout, tables, floats, absolute and relative positioning, the box model with box-sizing, collapsing margins, line breaking, pagination and the @page rules that decide the paper. Where it meets something it does not implement, it lays it out as a plain block and says so — the diagnostics list beside this preview is that report.

Where the numbers come from

Fidelity is measured rather than asserted. Every scenario in the corpus is rendered twice: once here, and once by Chromium's own printer. The two are compared as geometry and as pixels.
