Text in a shortened band

The panel to the right is a float. It is out of flow, so the heading above it keeps its full width and this paragraph keeps its own — what changes is the line boxes inside it, each shortened by the floats it overlaps.

Text is shaped rather than summed: advances come from rustybuzz, the shaper krilla already links, so kerning pairs and the fi and fl ligatures affect where a line breaks and not merely how it looks.

Match the browser’s construction, not its description — the pixels are the only thing that settles an argument.

Boxes, borders and backgrounds

The chart alongside is not an image. Each bar is a block whose percentage width resolves against the track beside its label, and the numbers are this page’s own: the column, the band the text above wraps into beside the panel, and the panel itself.
